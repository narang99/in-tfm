# Marin 32B layer 16 neuron 19311, as two reports: its gate row and its up row

Run on a Colab L4 (22.5 GB) on 2026-11-05.

## Question

- What does a Marin 32B MLP neuron look like in our clusters, against what the Marin atlas says about it?
- A SwiGLU neuron is `act = silu(gate) * up`, with 2 input rows.
  - Each row is linear in the MLP input, so each gets its own exact Hadamard and its own report.
  - `act` itself is not linear, so it gets no report here.

## The neuron

- Layer 16 is the deepest that fits the L4 with room for AttnLRP.
  - Layers 0 to 16 are about 18 GB in bf16.
- Neuron 19311 was picked from the atlas (`pilot/fineweb-10m-sparse`), for a clear meaning on its positive side.
  - Positive side, top 32 exemplars: "another", "more", "other", "further", "additional", "extra", "again".
  - Negative side, top 32: "continue", "yet", "same", "time", "else", and some "more", "further", "another".
  - Logit lens: no pattern, as expected 47 layers before the output.
  - `frac_pos` 0.50, max 2.89, min -3.84, `pos_q99` 0.19, `neg_q99` 0.37.
- No contributor has explained it in `Station-House/marin-neuron-hunt`. The atlas exemplars are the only ground truth.

## Code

- Branch `exp/marin-l16-19311`, on top of `feat/marin-32b`.
  - `feat/marin-32b` adds the Qwen3 adapter, which loads only the shards of layers 0 to 16.
  - It also fixes bf16 crashes, drops BOS from the hits for this model, and splits the runner into collect and report.
- Command, on Colab:

```
python scripts/marin_sweep_report.py --config configs/marin_l16_19311.yaml \
    --layer-name layers.16.mlp.gate_proj --out-dir reports/marin-32b/layers.16.mlp.gate_proj
python scripts/marin_sweep_report.py --config configs/marin_l16_19311.yaml \
    --layer-name layers.16.mlp.up_proj --out-dir reports/marin-32b/layers.16.mlp.up_proj
```

## Setup

- Corpus: all 10,000 `NeelNanda/pile-10k` documents, cut to 512 tokens.
  - 68% of the positions are real tokens, so about 3.5M positions.
  - The atlas used FineWeb, so the contexts differ. The words should not.
- Hits: 250,000, bucketed, from both tails.
- Clustering: HDBSCAN, leaf selection, on the L2-normalised Hadamards.
  - A sweep over (min_cluster_size, min_samples) runs on the same hits.
  - The report uses the lowest noise share, among settings with at most 1,500 clusters and a largest cluster of at most 25% of the hits.
- Report: 5 AttnLRP hits for each cluster.

## Checks before the run

- The truncated load has no missing weights.
  - transformers reports layers 17 to 63 and `lm_head` as unexpected, since they share shard files with the kept layers.
- Our `act` matches the atlas on 3 of its exemplars, run on the CPU from BOS:

| exemplar | atlas `act` | our `act` |
|---|---|---|
| negative side, rank 15, position 4 | -2.734 | -2.797 |
| negative side, rank 17, position 6 | -2.688 | -2.656 |
| negative side, rank 28, position 7 | -2.578 | -2.578 |

- A small run of 32 documents went through to AttnLRP.
  - About 2,000 tokens/s on the L4, and about 0.6 s for each AttnLRP hit.

## Results on pile-10k

- Both runs exited 0 and synced to `s3://narang99-private/in-tfm/marin-32b/layers.16.mlp.{gate_proj,up_proj}/19311`, without `hits.pt`.
- Scan and gather each took about 41 min, over 3,587,063 real positions. Pass 2 matched pass 1 to 0.031 (gate) and 0.023 (up), within the bf16 tolerance.

| row | negative hits | positive hits | chosen (mcs, ms) | clusters | noise | report |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| gate | 190,719 | 59,281 | (25, 8) | 294 | 93.0% | 962 s, 290 shown |
| up | 158,441 | 91,559 | (25, 8) | 390 | 91.5% | 1,277 s, 385 shown |

- The full sweeps are in each report's `sweep.json`. Every setting left 91% to 98% of hits as noise, with no cluster above 0.5% of the hits.
- Gate row, read from the 40 clusters with at least half their hits beyond the gate's own q99 (+1.345, -1.125):
  - The 9 opening clusters are all one concept: "again", "re-", "this time", "previous", "second", "another", "additional", "new", "otherwise".
  - The 31 closing clusters are mostly pile artefacts: code, JSON, license headers, PubMed, patents, math prompts.
  - A closed gate keeps `act` within -0.28 x `up`, so the closing clusters say where the neuron is off, not what it detects.
