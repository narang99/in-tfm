  • Hits enter from densest to sparsest over the kNN graph. When two components meet, the one with the lower peak dies.
  • Persistence is that peak's rho minus the rho where they met. A high value means a separate hill. A low value means a bump on one hill.
  • Rho is on a log10 scale, so a persistence of 0.3 means the peak is about twice as dense as the point where they met.
  • A threshold merges every peak with persistence up to it. The slider moves one threshold at a time, so each step holds at least one new merge.
  • Between trees, each side of a merge lists the densest distinct hits labelled with that tree, its root first.
  • Between all local modes, each side lists the densest distinct hits of that mode's basin, the hits that climb to it over this graph.
  • Between trees, only roots count as peaks, so a root's persistence ignores any denser hit that is no tree's root. A tree that absorbed one can look weaker here than the hill it sits on. The view between all modes has no such gap.
  • Tiles are the Hadamard vectors that were clustered, red for negative and green for positive. All tiles in one merge share a colour scale, so they compare across the two sides but not across merges.
  • Each merge between trees shows its route, from the merging root, through the merge point (thick border), to the other root.
  • A route is the fewest kNN hops over hits at least as dense as the merge point.
  • A route can cross other trees, since trees that merged earlier are already part of the hill. So its rho can dip and rise before the merge point, and each card names its own tree.
  • A long route is cut to 20 cards at one fixed step, counted out from the merge point. Both roots and the merge point always stay.
  • A tree's name links to its cluster in the report, with AttnLRP on each hit. A cluster holds only its tree's confident hits, and the report shows a random sample of them, so its hits can differ from the ones listed here.
  • Peaks in separate pieces of the graph never merge, at any threshold.
  • A peak marked "copies" is a group of more than k identical hits. Its density is a fixed top value, so its persistence is not a measurement, even when it is the largest on the page.
