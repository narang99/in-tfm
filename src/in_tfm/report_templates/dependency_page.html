How to read this page
Example
One hit of the cluster, a sample and the token the neuron fired at.
Dependency
One input dim of {{ origin.layer }}. Per example, the dims kept are the fewest, largest first, whose input × weight reach {{ "{:.0%}".format(selection.dim_mass) }} of the total |input × weight|.
Writer
What a dim is made of. A dim fed through a ReLU has one writer, a residual stream dim one per layer writing to it. Per firing, the writers kept are the ones reaching {{ "{:.0%}".format(selection.writer_mass) }} of the dim's |value|.
Noise units
(value − noise median) / (top of the noise's 90% shorth − median), against the writer's own noise at random positions. Past {{ settings.threshold }} is clearly above noise.
Contribution
A writer's part of the neuron's activation. Positive adds to it, negative inhibits it.
Share
Fired ratio × |median contribution|. Tables are ranked and cut by it, as each table's note says.
